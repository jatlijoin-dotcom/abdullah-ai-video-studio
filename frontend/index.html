Abdullah AI Video Studio

Aik kahani likhein — app us ka poora plan banata hai: scenes, camera, dialogue, narration, subtitles aur music. Jab provider key judi ho to aik final MP4 video banti hai jo phone par download ho jati hai.

Ye app kya karta hai

  • 2 modes: 3D Cartoon aur Realistic Cinematic
  • 3 zubanein: Urdu, Hindi, English (narration + dialogue)
  • 30 ya 60 second video — 9:16 (Shorts) ya 16:9
  • Character Lock: Abdullah har scene mein aik jaisa dikhta hai — wahi chehra, baal, daadhi, kala Levi's hoodie, neeli jeans, blue-grey sneakers
  • Alag awaazein: har character ki apni AI voice — dialogue narration se alag hota hai
  • Subtitles + music: Roman Urdu captions aur background music
  • Smooth transitions: scenes aik doosre se jud kar aik MP4 bante hain
  • Qeemat pehle: Generate se pehle poori cost table dikhti hai

Quick start (2 minute)

  1. Create tab kholein aur apni kahani likhein.
  2. Mode, duration, aspect, quality aur zubaan chunein.
  3. Qeemat dekhein dabayein — cost table dikhegi (Demo Mode mein bhi).
  4. Video banayein dabayein — progress dekhein, phir storyboard dekhein.
  5. Demo Mode mein demo plan download karein (ye video nahi hai). Asli MP4 sirf provider key ke saath banta hai.

Demo Mode kya hai?

Demo Mode mein koi provider key nahi hoti, is liye koi AI clip nahi banti aur koi paisa kharch nahi hota. Aap ko asli storyboard milta hai — har scene ka setting, action, camera, narration, dialogue (speaker + voice ke saath) aur video prompt — aur aik downloadable plan. Asli video ke liye Settings mein backend jorein aur provider key lagayein (approval ke baad).

Model: gemini-omni-1.2-flash (current). Veo 3.1 Preview band ho chuka hai (22 Oct 2026) — use nahi hota.
